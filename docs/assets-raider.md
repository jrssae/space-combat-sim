# `raider_fighter`: the raiders' Delta-V (M2-T04)

Generated by `tools/procgen/assets/raider_fighter.py` from `parts/wing.py`, the hump and nacelle parts and the
`raider` style (`tools/procgen/styles/raider.py`). Everything below refers to the default build (`seed 42`, spacing
0.09 m). Regenerate with `scripts/gen_assets.sh`; outputs land in `assets/generated/raider_fighter/` and are never
committed.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `raider_fighter.ply` | 26 488 Gaussian splats (budget 15k-40k), 3DGS binary PLY, GDGS orientation (Rz 180 of the Godot frame), the five chunks' splats concatenated in `manifest.counts` order |
| `raider_fighter.obj` + `.mtl` | fallback mesh, Godot frame; the game also uses it as the hull depth proxy (as for the Starfury, manifest `files.fallback_mesh`) |
| `raider_fighter_chunk_<name>.ply/.obj/.mtl` | the break-up chunks (below) |
| `hardpoints.json` | the contract for the flight model, weapons, collision / hit volumes, cameras, chunks (schema as `assets-starfury.md`, plus `chunks[]`) |
| `manifest.json` | `lods[0]`, AABB, `centroid_offset`, `files`, `counts` (per chunk), `chunks`, `sampling`, `dimensions_m` |

## What it is

A flying wing, worn grey-brown. From the maintainer's reference sheet (not in the repo) and
`docs/design-references.md`:

| Part | Authored frame (m, nose at z = 0, +Z aft) |
|---|---|
| Delta wing | half span 8.5 (17 m across), leading edge 56 deg from the centre line, root chord 5.6, square-cut tip chord 0.9 (tip trailing edge at z = 6.63), thickness 0.35 at the root tapering to 0.10 at the tips; darker plated edge band; two vent grilles, a few raised dark plates and an ochre band near each tip |
| Hump | superellipse loft, 1.7 m wide and 1.25 m high at most, from z = 0.5 to 5.4; dark opaque canopy panel on the forward slope (z 0.9-2.3); a louvre grille on the rear top |
| Nacelles | two drums at x = +-1.15, axis height 0.70, radius 0.70 (collar 0.78), z = 2.0 to 6.5 plus a 0.7 m bell nozzle (exit radius 0.55, Ø 1.1) ending at z = 7.2; a dark intake port in each rounded nose |
| Spike guns | four, 1.5 m long: two hung under the wing roots (x = +-3.0, muzzle z = 1.4), two on the nacelle flanks (x = +-2.07, muzzle z = 1.7) |

Overall: 7.2 m long, 17.0 m across, 2.0 m high, 20 000 kg (design mass, informational).

**Deviation from the written defaults.** The milestone's defaults (9 m long, leading edge 60 deg, Ø 1.1 nacelles at
x = +-0.9) were guesses made before the sheet arrived. The sheet shows a flatter ship: the span is about 2.7 times
the length, the leading edge about 54 deg from the centre line, the engine drums nearly flush with the trailing
edge and thick (about 2.2 m across the pair's outer extent). The generator follows the sheet: 56 deg, span / length
2.36, drums of Ø 1.4 at x = +-1.15 with the nozzle plane 0.6 m behind the wing's tip trailing edge. All of it is
a parameter in `RaiderSpec` / `WingSpec` / `NacelleSpec`. `(H)` judge AC15 against the sheet; T13 is the look pass
if it is rejected.

## Frames and groups

The same frames as the Starfury: authored Godot frame, then everything shifted so the **splat centroid is the
origin** (`manifest.centroid_offset` = (0, 0.256, 4.283) authored metres). Splats, OBJ and hardpoints share the
centred body frame. The sample set is exactly mirror-symmetric in x (right-hand groups sampled once and mirrored, the
hump symmetrised). Samples buried inside another part (wing under the hump and drums, drums under the wing and hump,
the hump under the wing) are dropped.

Every part belongs to one of five **groups**, in this order in the PLY and in `manifest.counts`:
`wing_l` (9 286 splats), `wing_r` (9 286), `hump` (2 066), `nacelle_l` (2 925), `nacelle_r` (2 925). Guns and their
pylons join the group they hang from.

## Chunks (`core/chunks.py`)

`hardpoints.json` `chunks[]`: `{name, ply, obj, offset, splats}` per group. Each chunk PLY and OBJ is re-centred on
the chunk's own splat centroid (GDGS centres every PLY on its mean position anyway) and the PLY is flipped like the
hull's, so a debris node at identity shows the chunk upright; `offset` is that centroid in the body frame. A chunk
placed at `ship transform * offset` sits exactly where its splats were: the chunks together are the hull's splats,
reproduced within 1e-5 m (tested). The mesh in the chunk OBJ is the group's fallback mesh in the same frame.
The routine is generic (`ChunkSource(name, splats, mesh)`), so M3 can chunk the Starfury.

## Hardpoints

- `pods`: `l` (left) and `r` (right), each `{center, main, retro}`: the main nozzle at the exhaust plane (z = 2.917
  centred, exhaust (0, 0, 1), radius 0.55) and a small retro nozzle in the nose (z = -2.283, exhaust (0, 0, -1), radius
  0.15). `ThrusterLayout` needs at least one pod per side; two pods work like four with fewer nozzles.
- `center_of_mass`: x = 0; y = the pods' nozzle y (0.44367), so pod thrust makes no pitch torque; z from the
  part masses (-0.154). All pod nozzle y equal the centre-of-mass y within 1e-6 (tested).
- `muzzles` (all direction (0, 0, -1)): `root_l`, `root_r`, `nacelle_l`, `nacelle_r`. Left and right take turns in
  alternating fire.
- `rcs`: six cosmetic ports (wing tips up and down, the leading edge outwards) with pilot-term tags from
  `pilot_tags`. They only drive plume glow.
- `collision` (also the hit volumes): `hump` (one box), `nacelle_l` / `nacelle_r` (capsules along z, radius = the
  collar), and five stepped boxes per wing half (`wing_r_step_0` ... `wing_l_step_4`) from the leading edge at the
  step's inner edge to the trailing edge at its outer edge. At least 97 % of the splat centres lie inside the
  primitives inflated by 0.15 m, and no primitive reaches more than 0.5 m past the splat AABB (tested, AC4).
- `cockpit_camera` under the canopy panel and `chase_anchor` (0, 3.24, 9.72) behind and above (the parser
  requires both; the AI raider uses neither).

## Style `raider`

Hull #A89F96, darker panels #544E48, dark recesses and vents #37312D, faded ochre `stripe` #B88A3A (an accent band, no
insignia), dark metal nozzles, a near-black **opaque** canopy. These are brighter than the milestone's "about #77706A":
the first capture on the real GPU (RX 6950 XT, Compute backend) showed that albedo as a nearly black silhouette,
because the baked studio lighting scales albedo by about 0.35 and the engine adds no light of its own. Panel grid 0.5 m, line width 3.5 cm, per-panel tint
jitter 0.12 (the Earthforce hull's is 0.05), so the plating reads patched and weathered. Left and right wings
get different panel phases on purpose (positions are symmetric, colours are not).

The baked light rig is the studio rig with a higher ambient (`RAIDER_RIG`: sky 0.46, ground 0.24, exposure 1.1 instead
of 0.30 / 0.10 / 1.0): in an unlit scene a surface facing away from the rig's key light would otherwise sit at 0.3 x
albedo, and raiders are mostly seen from behind or below.

## Determinism and options

As for the Starfury: `ctx.rng("splats", <part>)` streams, byte-identical output for the same seed. Options:
`spacing` (default 0.09 m; `--spacing`), `overlap`, `thickness`. The budget warning only applies at the default spacing.

`tools/procgen/tests/test_wing.py`, `test_chunks.py`, `test_raider_fighter.py` (files, budget, frames, symmetry,

## Known limits / for the human tester `(H)`

- How it looks is `(H)` (AC15). The splat spacing (0.09 m, sigma 0.126 m) suits the distances raiders are seen at; the
  panel detail is coarser than the Starfury's.
- The hump and drums are smooth lofts; the reference's are more angular and faceted. If the silhouette is right and the
  surface is not, that is the T13 look pass: better hump facets, drum ribbing, a grille inside each exhaust.
- The nacelle exhaust has no fan or grille inside it. The engine glow is a `ShipSpec` tint, not splats.
