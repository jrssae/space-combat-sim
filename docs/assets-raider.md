# `raider_fighter`: the raiders' Delta-V (M2-T04)

Generated by `tools/procgen/assets/raider_fighter.py` from `parts/raider.py` (the wing, the centre ridge and the engine
housings, M7-T16) and the `raider` style (`tools/procgen/styles/raider.py`). Everything below refers to the default build (`seed 42`, surface
samples at 0.09 m). Regenerate with `scripts/gen_assets.sh`; outputs land in `assets/generated/raider_fighter/` and are never
committed.

## What it is

A flying wing, worn grey-brown. Since M7-T16 the shape is the reference model's, `references/Raider_Fighter_16m.stl` (never
committed; read with `tools/refview`), scaled to 16 m across as its name gives: 7.37 m long, 16.0 m across, 1.53 m high. The model's
file axes map to the ship's as right = -x, up = +z, aft = +y; its nose is the delta's apex and its engines face the straight
trailing edge, as on the raider-base mood shot, where the two glows trail the straight edge. Measured sections (authored frame:
nose at z = 0, the wing's mid-plane at y = 0, which is the model's box centre plus 0.079 m):

| Part | Authored frame (m, nose at z = 0, +Z aft, wing plane y = 0) |
|---|---|
| Wing (`RaiderWing`) | half span 8.0; the leading edge one straight line x = 1.104 z from the nose to the tip (47.8 deg from the centre line), curling back into a pointed tip at (8.0, 7.37); a **notch** cut into it from x = 2.68 (its inner wall, z 2.43 to 3.10) out to where its back wall (z = 3.10) meets the edge at x = 3.42; the trailing edge straight at z = 7.374 from the tip in to x = 2.65, then chamfered in to z = 6.80 at x = 2.10 and straight to the root. Section: sharp at the leading edge, 0.18 m thick 0.2 m behind it, thickening 0.09 m per metre to 0.34 m, closing to 0.10 m at the trailing edge (0.8 m per metre); outboard of x = 6.4 it thins to 0.07 m at the tip. The notch's walls cut through the thick wing |

The dark opaque canopy lies along its top over the nose (z 1.15 to 2.95) |
| Housings (`RaiderLobe`, the `nacelle` groups) | an engine housing either side of the ridge, z 3.70 to 6.80: flat above and below (0.68 / -0.61 at their fullest, 6 cm higher and lower by the ridge), the outer side rounding over 0.8 m into the wing at x = 2.44; a grilled intake (dark vanes 0.1 m apart, z 3.15 to 3.70) ahead of a dark front face; behind, the housing drops at z = 6.1 to a low rear face with the main nozzle (x = 1.07, exit Ø 0.34, ending z = 6.92) and a small dark tail pod (x = 1.87, to z = 6.97) |
| Top detail | two vent grilles, three raised plates and the ochre band, each well inside the planform (0.1 m behind the leading edge at least) |

Overall: 7.37 m long, 16.0 m across, 1.51 m high, 20 000 kg (design mass, informational; the flight model's mass and inertia are
`assets/ships/raider_delta_v.tres`'s).

**Before M7-T16** the raider followed a reference sheet without a model (M2-T04): 7.2 m by 17 m, 2.0 m high, a 56 deg leading
edge, a smooth hump with two round drums on top, and raised plates and vents that overhung the leading edge as stepped blocks
("the panels leading the wing are really wrong", the maintainer). The scores of that build and this one are under "Fidelity".

## Frames and groups

The same frames as the Starfury: authored Godot frame, then everything shifted so the **surface samples' centroid is the
origin** (`manifest.centroid_offset` about (0, 0.036, 4.709) authored metres). The meshes and hardpoints share the
centred body frame. The sample set is exactly mirror-symmetric in x (right-hand groups sampled once and mirrored, the
ridge symmetrised). Samples buried inside another part (wing under the ridge and housings, housings inside the wing and ridge,
the ridge inside the wing and housings) are dropped.

Every part belongs to one of five **groups**, in this order: `wing_l`, `wing_r`, `hump` (the ridge and canopy), `nacelle_l`,
`nacelle_r` (the housings). The guns join their wing's group.

## Chunks (`core/chunks.py`)

`hardpoints.json` `chunks[]`: `{name, obj, offset, mesh}` per group. Each chunk's OBJ is re-centred on the group's surface samples'
centroid; `offset` is that centroid in the body frame, so a chunk placed at `ship transform * offset` sits where its parts were. Its
textured mesh is the hull's faces of the group on the hull's UVs (M4-T30d), so chunks draw with the hull's materials.

## Hardpoints

- `pods`: `l` (left) and `r` (right), each `{center, main, retro}`: the main nozzle at its exit on each housing's back (x = 1.07,
  z = 2.211 centred, exhaust (0, 0, 1), radius 0.17) and the retro at the intake grille's front (z = -1.559, exhaust (0, 0, -1),
  radius 0.15). `ThrusterLayout` needs at least one pod per side; two pods work like four with fewer nozzles.
- `center_of_mass`: x = 0; y = the pods' nozzle y (-0.03628, the wing plane), so pod thrust makes no pitch torque; z from the
  part masses (-0.277). All pod nozzle y equal the centre-of-mass y within 1e-6 (tested).
- `muzzles` (all direction (0, 0, -1), z = -2.009, on the wing plane): `root_l`, `root_r` (the inner notch guns, x = -+2.84),
  `nacelle_l`, `nacelle_r` (the outer ones, x = -+3.16). The names are the contract's from before M7-T16, when two guns hung on the
  drums; left and right take turns in alternating fire.
- `rcs`: six cosmetic ports (wing tips up and down, the leading edge outwards 4 m out) with pilot-term tags from
  `pilot_tags`. They only drive plume glow.
- `collision` (also the hit volumes): `hump` (one box, 0.9 by 1.52 by 7.0 m), `nacelle_l` / `nacelle_r` (one box each, 2.24 by
  1.42 by 3.77 m, grille to nozzle), and five stepped boxes per wing half (`wing_r_step_0` ... `wing_l_step_4`, 1.6 m strips) from the
  leading edge's line at the step's inner edge to the trailing edge, as thick as the wing's thickest section in the strip (0.34 m;
  0.28 at the tip). At least 97 % of the surface samples lie inside the primitives inflated by 0.15 m, and no primitive reaches more
  than 0.5 m past the samples' box (tested, AC4). Before M7-T16 the drums were capsules (r 0.78) and the steps 1.7 m strips.
- `cockpit_camera` under the canopy and `chase_anchor` behind and above (the parser
  requires both; the AI raider uses neither).

## Style `raider`

Hull #A89F96, darker panels #544E48, dark recesses and vents #37312D, faded ochre `stripe` #B88A3A (an accent band, no
insignia), dark metal nozzles, a near-black **opaque** canopy. These are brighter than the milestone's "about #77706A":
the first capture on the real GPU (RX 6950 XT, Compute backend) showed that albedo as a nearly black silhouette, and raiders
are mostly seen on their dark side. Panel grid 0.5 m, line width 3.5 cm, per-panel tint
jitter 0.12 (the Earthforce hull's is 0.05), so the plating reads patched and weathered. Left and right wings
get different panel phases on purpose (positions are symmetric, colours are not).

The game lights the textured hull from the scene (`docs/architecture.md` section 7.3).

### Metal, not plastic (M7-T16)

The maintainer (2026-11-13): "the raider must look metallic, not plastic". Until generator 6 the paint was one flat roughness
(`paint_roughness` 0.82, metallic 0): matte plastic. The colours stay as they were (the albedo maps are byte-identical); only the
ORM map's roughness (green) and metal (blue) change, and the normal map keeps T15's option-A panel relief. `MeshAssets` reads
both channels as before, with the `hull_sheen` clear coat on top.

| Material (role) | Where | Metallic | Roughness |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rd_hull` (hull) | the painted plating: wing skins, ridge | 0.45 | 0.50 |
| `rd_hull_panel` (hull_panel) | darker panels, the wing's edge walls, vent backings, raised plates | 0.50 | 0.46 |
| `rd_hull_metal` (hull_metal, new) | the engine housings: bare metal, the hull's colour | 0.72 | 0.34 |
| `rd_hull_dark` (hull_dark) | louvre slats, intake vanes, dark end faces, tail pods | 0.75 | 0.36 |
| `rd_stripe`, `rd_hazard` | the ochre band | 0.35 | 0.55 |
| `rd_hazard_dark` | (unused on the raider) | 0.30 | 0.60 |
| `rd_nozzle` | nozzle bells | 0.90 | 0.28 |
| `rd_nozzle_throat` | the glowing throats | 0.80 | 0.45 |
| `rd_barrel` | gun barrels | 0.90 | 0.26 |
| `rd_bore` | the bores | 0.50 | 0.60 |
| `rd_canopy` | the opaque canopy | 0.00 | 0.12 |
| `rd_canopy_frame`, `rd_rcs` | (frames, RCS ports) | 0.60, 0.80 | 0.40, 0.35 |

On top of the materials, in both texture sets (`style.extra["metal_finish"]`, `textures.metal_finish`, opt-in per style):

- **Per panel:** the painted materials' roughness steps by up to +-0.12 from one 0.9 m cell to the next and drifts +-0.06 within
  it (0.35 m noise), so a highlight breaks up instead of running across the wing. In the high set the drawn plates add their
  own +-0.12 per plate (the layouts' `sheen`, 0.07 to 0.08 before).
- **Edges:** chipped edges are bare metal (roughness -0.25, metallic up to 0.85), as before.
- **Dirt:** streaks, smudges and the darker half of the grime are up to 0.3 rougher and up to half as metallic; soot behind the
  nozzles is 0.3 rougher, as before; seams 0.25 rougher.

## Surface (M7-T15)

The hull's surface is drawn per part from the maintainer's references (`references/T40 seam references/`: `raider close up`,
`raider and wagon close up`, and the raider carrier and battle wagon close-ups for the raiders' construction; never committed,
other artists' renders, inspiration only, nothing reused or traced). The layouts are `layouts.raider(...)` in
`tools/procgen/parts/layouts.py`, sized from the wing's own sweep, chords and span and the nacelles' axis
(`raider_fighter.surface_layouts(spec)`), baked as the civilians' are (architecture §18.30) with the generic plating off.
Meshes, chunks, hardpoints, the emissive map and the raider's paint (hull, panels, ochre accent, the belly's weathering) do not
change.

- **Read, wing.** A band of plating runs inside the leading edge, round the tip and along the trailing edge, and the leading
  edge carries a row of teeth. Inside the band each bay between the wing's frames holds a trapezoid panel outlined parallel
  to the bay's sides, often with a smaller one inside it. Louvre banks sit by the root, a long hatch near the trailing edge.
- **Read, hump and nacelles.** The hump has frames behind the canopy, a belt along each flank and small hatches and louvres
  low on the sides. The engine housings are ribbed cages: close rings and longitudinal strakes. The carrier and battle wagon
  show the same salvaged construction: patched plates of mixed size and shade over a regular frame.
- **High set (`texture_detail = high`, the default; the maintainer's option A, 2026-11-13).** Wing, top and belly: the edge
  band 0.55 m inside the leading edge and 0.3 m inside the trailing edge, the two frames (x 3.0 and 5.7 m), nineteen teeth
  along the leading edge, a trapezoid panel inside each of the three bays and a smaller one inside the outer two, a louvre
  bank by the root and a long hatch aft on top, a hatch and a slatted panel underneath; large plates (2.2 by 1.35 m) in 7 %
  tone with a few small proud hatches. Hump: two frames behind the canopy, a third aft, the flank belt, a hatch and a louvre
  low on each side, plates of mixed size in 7 % tone. Nacelles: end rings, three ribs, twelve strakes, plates in 7 % tone.
- **Low set (`texture_detail = low`: major seams only).** The edge band's line on top and belly, the wing's two frames, the
  hump's two frames behind the canopy, the nacelle's two end rings. Written as `raider_fighter_{albedo,normal,orm}_low.png`,
  named in every surface's `detail.low`; the glow map is shared.
- **Sheet.** `references/screenshots/M7-T15 surface sheets/M7-T15_options_raider.png` (the choice: current, low, A, B, C;
  RX 6950 XT, Forward+) and the as-built low and high frames under `final/`. Generator version 5.
- **On the M7-T16 shape (generator 6).** The same reading, refitted: `layouts.raider(le_ratio, half_span, trailing_z, notch,
  body_x, lobe_z, lobe_x, canopy_z)`. Wing: the edge band runs from the housing's edge round the notch's walls, the tip and the
  trailing edge; the frames stand at the notch's outer corner (x 3.42) and 5.7 m, both behind the notch; ten teeth outboard of
  the notch; two bays (a panel in each and a smaller one inside), the louvre bank behind the notch, the hatches moved inside the
  new planform. Ridge (the `hump` layout): frames just ahead of and behind the canopy, two more over the hump, a flank belt, a
  hatch and a louvre low on each side, plates. Housings (the `nacelle` layout): end rings, three ribs, three strakes along the top
  and belly, a belt round the outer side, plates. Low set: the band's line, the two frames, the ridge's two frames, the housings'
  end rings. Paint, the belly's weathering and the emissive map unchanged.

## Fidelity (M7-T16)

The raider is held to the civilians' 90 % rule (M5 Q18, `docs/assets-civilian.md` "Fidelity"): top, side and front silhouette
IoU at least 0.90 each and length, width and height each within 10 % of the model at the same length. The model is scaled to
16 m across (7.374 m long); the authored frame's wing plane sits 0.079 m above the model's box centre, so the run passes that
offset (without it the tool's 2 % shift finds the same place: 0.992 / 0.979 / 0.965):

```
.venv/bin/python3 -I tools/refview/compare.py references/Raider_Fighter_16m.stl raider_fighter --length 7.374 \
    --right=-x --up=+z --aft=+y --model-offset 0,0.079,0
```

| Build | Top IoU | Side IoU | Front IoU | Length | Width | Height | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Generator 5 (M2-T04 to M7-T15) | 0.605 | 0.513 | 0.506 | 0.976 (7.2 m) | 1.062 (17.0 m) | 1.316 (2.0 m) | fail |
| Generator 6 (M7-T16) | **0.992** | **0.982** | **0.969** | 1.000 (7.374 m) | 1.000 (16.0 m) | 0.992 (1.51 m) | pass |

What differed, part by part (generator 5 against the model), and what generator 6 does:

1. **The panels along the wing's leading edge.** Four raised plates, two vents and the ochre band were placed by span, not by the
   edge: at 56 deg they reached up to 0.4 m ahead of the leading edge, a staircase of blocks standing in the air in the top
   view. Now every detail part sits inside the planform (tested: 0.1 m behind the edge at least) and on the skin's lowest point
   under it.
2. **The leading edge.** 56 deg from the centre line and straight; the model's is 47.8 deg, one line from the nose to the tip,
   with the notch where the guns sit. Now as the model's.
3. **The wing.** 17 m across, square-cut 0.9 m tips, the trailing edge raked from z 5.6 at the root to 6.6 at the tip, 0.35 to
   0.10 m thick tapering out. The model: 16 m, pointed tips, a straight trailing edge at 7.37 chamfered in at the root, a section
   thickening aft to 0.33 m all along the span. Now as the model's.
4. **The hump and drums.** A smooth hump 1.7 m wide standing 1.25 m above the wing and two Ø 1.4 m drums on top of it, all above
   the wing (the ship 2.0 m high). The model's body sits across the wing, as much below as above: a narrow ridge and keel
   (+0.84 / -0.68) between two flat engine housings 2.2 m wide each, intakes in front, exhausts in a low rear face. Now so.
5. **The engines.** Big bells (Ø 1.1) behind the trailing edge's line; the model's exhausts sit in the housings' rear faces
   ahead of the trailing edge. Now Ø 0.34 nozzles there.
6. **The cockpit.** A dark panel on the hump's front slope; the model's canopy is the ridge's top over the nose. Now there.
7. **The guns.** Four 1.5 m spikes under the wing roots and on the drums' flanks; the model's are two short guns in each notch.
   Now so (the muzzles keep their names).

**Sheet** (for the maintainer, `(H)`): `references/screenshots/M7-T16 raider STL/M7-T16_raider_stl_sheet.png`, the old and new
silhouettes against the model with their scores and GPU frames of both (RX 6950 XT, Forward+); the raw frames under `raw/`.

## Determinism and options

As for the Starfury: `ctx.rng("surface", <part>)` streams, byte-identical output for the same seed. Options:
`spacing` (default 0.09 m, the surface samples'), `render_triangles`, `atlas_px`, `texel_m`, `occlusion`.

`tools/procgen/tests/test_chunks.py`, `test_raider_fighter.py` (files, frames, symmetry, chunks, hardpoints contract, nozzle y =

## Known limits / for the human tester `(H)`

- How it looks is `(H)` (AC15). The atlas is at about 1.5 cm a texel; the panel detail is coarser than the Starfury's.
- The ridge and housings are smooth lofts; the model's are more angular, its canopy ridge has two raised blades either side of a
  channel and its rear is an open frame round the engines. The silhouettes pass; those are surface work if the maintainer wants it.
- The exhausts have no grille inside them. The plumes' glow is a `ShipSpec` tint; the throats glow in the emissive map.
- `DamageZones` (HUD) still splits the delta at |x| = 1.8 m; the housings now reach 2.44 m, so a hit on a housing's outer rim
  counts to its wing.
