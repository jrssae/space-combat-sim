### M13.5-T01 Promotion to squadron leader

A pilot who misses the tutorial's qualification (an exemplary buoy course, no more than one Starfury and no civilian lost)
joins the squadron, and a later mission (M12 or M13, Q1) promotes them to leader. The rank is saved with the career profile
(D79) and survives restarts.

### M13.5-T02 The docking bay tunnel in deep red

Babylon 5's main docking bay tunnel lit and coloured **deep red** as in the show; it renders white/grey today (found by
M8-T31's shot review). The station generator's lamps and materials; a look sheet for the maintainer first (H).

### M13.5-T03 Turbulent engine plumes

Engine plumes with a moving, cloud-like turbulence (maintainer, 2026-11-25; the design is in `docs/roadmap.md`, "Later, and
not planned"): a plume shader on today's cone and core (`ThrusterVisuals`, `assets/materials/thruster_plume.tres`), noise
scrolling along the exhaust, brighter and denser at the nozzle, fading at the tip, its speed and brightness from the throttle,
the noise phase from the per-entity hash so ships do not pulse together. The instanced plumes keep one draw call per ship;
fill rate is measured on the GPU in a fleet fight (a cheaper far setting if needed). The look is the maintainer's (H).

### M13.5-T04 Wing-level AI as a feel trial

The wing picks targets and tactics a few times a second and its members steer, avoid and aim every tick (M4-T32's option B,
Q51, `docs/research/wing-level-ai.md`), starting from "the wing picks targets" on `WingDirector`. It saves almost nothing in
performance, so it is kept only if the maintainer judges the dogfights feel better (H); otherwise the task becomes `not
needed`.

### M13.5-T05 Fleet-level AI

A fleet director per faction above the wings and capitals (M10-T80's ITF review, "Faction doctrine and fleet-level
behavior", `docs/research/itf-feature-review.md`): data-driven doctrine per faction (patrol strength, threat response,
retreat thresholds, interception range, carrier reserve) that assigns wings and capitals to patrols, intercepts, escorts and
defensive launches, and tells the player why in a short line (for example, a patrol launched because a sector has too few
defenders). It hands orders to the wings, so it builds on T04 if T04 is kept. Per-ship per-tick work stays in the native core
(architecture §17). Seeded balance runs on the main machine, never on `oscar`. Scope from Q2 to Q5.

### M13.5-T06 New game screen flybys

Behind the New game list (`NewGameSelect`, today over the hangar backdrop), two in-engine flybys of Babylon 5 with our own
models, lights and sky: one staged after the **season 2 opening titles' station flyby**, one after the **season 3
cast-introduction station flyby**. Re-staged from a written shot list (camera paths, timing, framing) added to
`docs/design-references.md`. No footage, music, narration audio, title cards or logos from the show (`DISCLAIMER.md`); the
season 3 flyby introduces the game's own characters (the instructor, the wing, station roles) as name cards, never actor
likenesses. Played on the `CINEMATIC` camera; the menu stays usable with the pad throughout.

**Questions first.** Before any build work, the agent asks the maintainer for references to both sequences, drafts the shot
list from them, and sends its clarification questions with the draft: which flyby plays when (alternate, random, one per
session), skippable or always looping, music or ambience, which characters the season 3 cards name. It waits for the answers.

### M13.5-T07 Menu language cleanup

Every player-facing string in the front end, settings pages (`SettingsSchema` labels and the help shown in menus), controls
menu, pause menu, loading screen, debrief and the New game list, rewritten in plain player language: no internal names (tier,
Jolt, rows, task ids such as "M5-T17"), one capitalisation style (Q6), consistent verbs and units. An inventory of today's
strings and the proposed wording goes to the maintainer first (H); a GUT test then keeps task ids and internal terms out of
menu text. The controls menu's key names follow T08.

### M13.5-T08 Keyboard controls cleanup

The keyboard and mouse layout (layout B in `InputAdapter`, the default control profile's keyboard events) reviewed for
clashes, keys that are hard to reach while the right hand flies the mouse, actions the pad has and the keys lack, and how the
keys show in the controls menu and the tutorial's prompts.

**Layout view first.** The agent shows the maintainer a keyboard layout view of today's bindings (a drawn keyboard with each
key labelled by its action, the mouse buttons beside it), then asks what to change; the changes are settled with the
maintainer during the task, not before it (H), including whether the old layout stays as a saved profile. The tutorial's
control checks keep reading actions, never keys (D80), and the ` key stays the console's.
