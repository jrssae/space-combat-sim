## Goal

Bring the setting's fleets into the game. Every capital warship in the maintainer's references is generated by code from
its reference model and show images, flies as a Newtonian body under the native core, and fights with its faction's
weapons: beams on most hulls, interceptors on some (mostly Earth Alliance), turrets, missiles and fighters launched from
its bays. Capital ships take damage by sections: they lose turrets, engines and bays, become disabled hulks that drift
on their last motion, or break up in stages with secondary blasts that hurt whatever is close. Every explosion becomes a
blast with distance falloff. The factions (Earth Alliance, Earthforce renegades, raiders, Narn, Centauri, Minbari, Drazi,
Brakiri, Vree, the Interstellar Alliance, Shadows, Vorlons, Drakh) are data, each with its own style, fighters and
weapons, and the Shadows and Vorlons bring the show's mechanics. M4's benchmark stand-ins go, replaced by the real ships.

## Design (to go into `docs/architecture.md`, in the section after M11's)

### D12.1 Capital assets

- One generator per hull in `tools/procgen/assets/` (for example `ea_omega.py`), on a shared capital parts library in
  `tools/procgen/parts/` (hull sections, plating, turret and beam emitter mounts, interceptor domes, launch bays and tubes,
  engine blocks, rotating sections, antennae) and a style per faction in `tools/procgen/styles/`.
- The output contract every asset meets (§14.8, `docs/assets-civilian.md`), plus:
  - **sections** (`manifest.sections[]`): a name, a kind (`reactor`, `magazine`, `fuel`, `bridge`, `hangar`, `engine`,
    `habitat`, `structure`), its share of hull points, its convex pieces and hit volumes, what it holds (hardpoints), and
    for the explosive kinds a blast energy (D12.6);
  - **hardpoints** for every weapon kind (turrets, beam emitters, interceptors, missile launchers, mine layers), launch bays
    and tubes (a fighter's start pose and exit direction), engines (thrust direction and share), rotating parts, lights;
  - **collision** from M7's builder (D73), one set of convex pieces per section, so the break-up's chunks keep collision;
  - **break-up chunks** cut along the sections' boundaries on the hull's atlas (`core/chunks.py`).
- Texel tiers as M7's station (D72): per-piece atlases for the broad paint and a shared detail layer tiled in world space,
  so a 1 to 3 km hull reads from 50 m; VRAM recorded per hull.
- Low and high texture sets per hull (M5-T40's setting); far representations as M6's D70.
- The 90 % compare against the STL (Q10), scores in `docs/assets-capital.md`.

### D12.2 Organic hulls and sheen

- `tools/procgen/core/sdf.py` (signed distance fields, smooth unions, metaballs, spline-swept bodies, noise displacement)
  meshed to the textured mesh writer's input, with budgets and determinism tests like every part.
- A view-dependent sheen layer over the four maps, as the liveries are a layer: spherical-harmonic iridescence for the
  Vorlons, the Shadows' black hulls with their faint purple sheen and moving surface patterns; one shader include, its
  parameters per style.
- The Shadows, Vorlons and Drakh are organic; the White Star and the Victory-class mix Minbari and Vorlon lines
  (hard-surface parts with organic fairings).

### D12.3 Factions as data

- `data/factions.json` grows to `earth_alliance`, `renegades`, `raiders`, `narn`, `centauri`, `minbari`, `drazi`,
  `brakiri`, `vree`, `isa`, `shadows`, `vorlons`, `drakh` and `neutral`, with Q12's relations; scenarios override them.
- Renegades fly EA hulls with their own marking (a livery layer, as M4-T30b's wing liveries), chosen at spawn.
- A faction names its style, its fighters, its weapon kinds and its mine kind; hulls read them from data, not code.

### D12.4 Capital ships in the game

- A capital-scale `ShipSpec` (mass, inertia, thrust from the engine hardpoints, turn rates, `can_open_jump_point`, the
  sections) flown through the native flight step; a `RigidBody3D` with zero damping (`DAMP_MODE_REPLACE`), forces
  re-applied every tick; compound collision from the manifest; rotating sections as `AnimatableBody3D` (the Omega's drum).
- Hit volumes per section in the native core, so the hit sweep, the rams and the turrets' line of fire (Q33) see the real
  shape; `Targetable` kind `CAPITAL`, broadcast range by class; the low-attention tier carries capitals and hulks as rows.
- Jump capability per class (Q22): the larger classes open points, the list from T01 and T03.
- `CapitalStandin` and its box hulls (`capital_hulls`) are removed once the bench draws real hulls (T45).

### D12.5 Sections and subsystems

- A hit lands on the section its hit volume belongs to (M4's `DamageZones`, M7's section damage). A section lost takes
  what it holds with it: its turrets, beams and interceptors stop, an engine section's thrust share is gone, a hangar's
  bays stop launching, a bridge lost cuts control, a reactor lost cuts power in stages.
- The ship is **disabled** (D12.7) when power or control is gone and no explosive section has blown; it is **destroyed**
  when a reactor, magazine or fuel section blows (D12.8).
- Subsystems are targetable (Q17): the radial target menu's second level lists a capital's live subsystems.

### D12.6 The blast model

- One native kernel for every blast: mines, missiles, torpedoes, explosive sections and every ship's death. A blast has an
  energy and a radius; the damage to a hull falls smoothly to zero at the radius, measured from the blast's centre to the
  hull's nearest hit volume, and the same falloff gives the push on nearby bodies. T09 picks the curve and pins it in tests.
- A ship's death is a blast scaled by its class and what is aboard (Q18), replacing the placeholders M4 left on
  `HitInfo` (1e5 J a kilogram of hull, four bounding radii; §17.14). M4-T23's far flashes read the same energy.
- Run in the tick on the main thread like the hit sweep, results identical with 1 and 6 threads; M4's flat 200-point mine
  blast is replaced.

### D12.7 Disabled hulks

- A disabled ship loses power, control and thrust in stages, stops fighting and drifts on its last motion (Newtonian, no
  damping). Its brain is released; it stays `Targetable` and its sections can still be hit.
- In the tier it is a `FLAG_HULK` row (§17.14), never removed for being idle; Q19's cap scuttles the oldest.
- A jump point it held collapses (M6-T23's rule); an explosive section hit later blows it up (D12.8).

### D12.8 Staged break-up

- A capital's death is a plan, not one effect: secondary blasts walk from the explosive section outward, sections come
  apart as chunks on their own motion (M3's `StagedWreck` and `BreakupPlan`, generalised from the Starfury to sections),
  fires and venting, the reactor's blast last; each blast is a D12.6 blast and a far flash.
- Explosions pooled with shared meshes and materials (M4's review item L10).

### D12.9 Weapons

- **Beams** as native rows with no node per shot (M4-T20 measured about 20 us a beam shot through a `Projectile` node
  against a 1 us budget): pulsed beams (one tick) and sustained beams that sweep a hull for a duration (the Shadows'
  slicer, the Vorlons' lightning per T04), drawn in one batch per kind.
- **Interceptors** (mostly EA, Q9): a native kernel that shoots down incoming bolts and missiles near its ship at a rate,
  replacing M4's stand-in.
- **Turrets** on M5's native turret rows (§18.10) with new capital kinds; **missiles** on M4-T20's native guidance path;
  **mines** as native rows (§17.16) with D12.6's blast, placed by data (Q20) and laid in battle by the hulls T03 gives
  mine layers.
- Each weapon is a `WeaponSpec` in `assets/weapons/`; every kind's per-tick work is in the native core, threads identical,
  parity recordings green. M4's turret, beam, missile and mine stand-ins and their counters (L11) are removed in T45.

### D12.10 Capital AI and carriers

- The `capital` brain profile (Q16, its behaviour settled in T03) runs in the native AI job one tick behind like every brain; its commands are applied at
  the join against the pilot's stamp.
- Fighters gain attack runs on capitals and their subsystems, and keep out of beam arcs.
- Carriers launch their faction's fighters from their bays and tubes (D12.1's hardpoints) through the ship pool, replacing
  M4's stand-in launches; M7's launch sequence for AI ships is reused where it fits. Recovery into a bay is out.

### D12.11 The ancients' and special mechanics

- **To be settled with the maintainer in T04** and written here (Q6: the full show mechanics). The list T04 starts from:
  Shadow phasing in and out, Shadow jamming (hard to lock for younger races), the slicer beam, the Shadows' and Vorlons'
  self-repair, the Vorlon lightning beam, how Shadow and Vorlon fighters fight, the ancients' arrivals, the Drakh's
  Shadow-derived technology, Minbari stealth (Q13: milder than the Shadows' jamming), and the show's counters to them.
- Fixed by the architecture whatever T04 decides: per-tick work in the native core, threads identical, gameplay through
  `FlightCommand` and `CombatCommand`, no wall clock.

### D12.12 The player at a capital's helm

- A sandbox and console option puts the player in command of a capital ship (Q15). `InputAdapter` writes its
  `FlightCommand` as for a fighter; the capital's spec limits the response. A camera for a 1 to 3 km hull (chase distance and
  view from its size).
- AI gunners fire every weapon. The player gives them **firing orders** from a radial menu on M5's radial framework
  (`UiCommand`, never `Input`): hold fire, fire at will, target weapons only and the others T03 adds (for example return fire
  only, focus my target, engines only, point defence only). The orders are data the gunners read at the join, so the AI's
  capitals can use the same orders.
- Gameplay never reads `Input`; the tutorial's actions are unchanged.
