# `starfury_aurora`: the player's ship (M1-T04)

Generated by `tools/procgen/assets/starfury_aurora.py` from reusable parts (`tools/procgen/parts/`) and the
`earthforce` style (`tools/procgen/styles/earthforce.py`). Everything below refers to the default build
(`seed 42`, spacing 0.035 m). Regenerate with `scripts/gen_assets.sh`; outputs land in
`assets/generated/starfury_aurora/` and are never committed.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `starfury_aurora.ply` | 136 682 Gaussian splats, 3DGS binary PLY (SH degree 0), written in GDGS orientation (Rz 180 of the Godot frame) |
| `starfury_aurora.obj` + `.mtl` | fallback mesh, Godot frame, smooth vertex normals, faces grouped by material |
| `cockpit_interior.obj` + `.mtl` | base frame ring, two canopy hoops, dash slab; shown in first person only |
| `hardpoints.json` | the contract for the flight model, cameras, weapons, RCS glow and collision (schema below) |

## Frames

1. **Authored frame**: Godot axes (+X right, +Y up, -Z forward), metres, fuselage axis on x = y = 0, z = 0 at
   the fuselage mid-length. Parts are placed here and read back for anchors.
2. **Balanced**: the sample set is made *exactly* mirror-symmetric (centre-line parts keep their x > 0
   samples and mirror them; the pod/strut cross is sampled once and mirrored four ways), then the fuselage
   group (fuselage, canopy, tub, spine, fairing, barrels) is shifted in y by a few tenths of a millimetre so
   the splat centroid's y is exactly 0. The pods are therefore symmetric about both x = 0 and y = 0 in the
   final frame, and the thrust axis passes through the origin.
3. **Centred**: the sample mean z (1.6757 m at seed 42, `manifest.centroid_offset`) is subtracted from
   everything (splats, meshes, hardpoints), so the written splat centroid is (0, 0, 0) to float32 precision
   and GDGS's unconditional import centring is a no-op. The origin therefore moves by a few millimetres
   between seeds; all x/y values and all z *differences* are seed-independent.
4. **PLY only**: positions, normals and rotations are pre-rotated by Rz(180 deg) (`core/frames.py`) so the
   `GaussianSplatNode` stays at identity. Meshes and hardpoints stay in the Godot frame. The manifest AABB is
   the Godot-frame box of the written splat centres (apply the flip to compare with the imported resource).

## Splats: how the hull is sampled and why

The top rendering risk is a "dotty" hull in the cockpit view (`docs/architecture.md` section 7.4). The
choices below are what keeps the surface continuous; `core/sampling.py` has the details.

- **Area-weighted, stratified.** Each face gets `area / spacing^2` samples in expectation; the fractional
  part is rounded stochastically (`floor(expected + u)`) so the expectation is exact and tiny faces are not
  all dropped. Density is identical on a hull panel and on a 5 cm RCS cone.
- **Low discrepancy inside faces.** Points come from the R2 quasi-random sequence mapped to each triangle
  with Heitz's low-distortion square-to-triangle map, with a random offset per face. The nearest-neighbour
  spacing is tight (median 0.57 s, 1st percentile 0.2 s; a random pattern gives 0.45 s with many clumps and
  holes), so the overlap needed to close the sheet is small and predictable.
- **Flat, oriented Gaussians.** Rotation from the interpolated vertex normal (smooth on curved parts, hard
  on boxes), in-plane sigma `k s` with `k = 1.4`, normal sigma `0.03 s` (about 1 mm): a thin disc that blends
  with its neighbours into a sheet instead of a cloud of balls. Measured on the fuselage and a pod at
  s = 0.035 m, the composite opacity of the sheet at independent test points (alpha 0.98, worst 0.1
  percentile / minimum): k = 0.8 -> 0.77 / 0.46, k = 1.0 -> 0.98 / 0.89, k = 1.2 -> 0.999 / 0.994,
  **k = 1.4 -> 1.000 / 0.9999**. The architecture's k = 1.4 is therefore kept as the default; it costs
  about 12 overlapping splats per pixel (early-out keeps that cheap) and blurs edges by roughly 2 sigma
  (0.1 m). `--option overlap=1.2` is the first thing to try if the tester wants crisper panel lines.
- **Spacing 0.035 m, not 0.06 m.** Section 9.1 estimated "0.06 m -> ~150k points", but this ship has
  about 167 m^2 of surface, so 0.06 m gives only 45k splats, below the AC3 budget of 100k-250k. The default
  spacing is 0.035 m -> 136 682 splats (9.3 MB), within budget and finer for the close-up nose. `--spacing`
  overrides it; the manifest records what was used.
- **Baked studio light** (`core/lighting.py`): hemisphere ambient (sky 0.30, ground 0.10) plus a warm key
  from upper front right (0.72), a cool fill from lower left rear (0.22) and a rim from above and behind
  (0.18), with mild wrap. The hull reads ~0.95 x albedo on top and ~0.3 x underneath; nothing is black. No
  view-dependent terms (SH degree 0 cannot hold them and a baked highlight is wrong for every other camera).
  Lighting rotates with the ship; a real sun needs GDGS relighting later (section 7.3).
- **Surface detail** (`styles/`): paneled materials get a world-aligned 0.6 m panel grid projected along the
  axis closest to the normal, 4 cm panel lines at 55 % albedo and a +-5 % per-panel tint from a cell hash
  (deterministic, no rng). The pod stripe and the dark bands are materials on the geometry, so they also
  appear in the OBJ.
- **Canopy.** The cockpit camera sits inside the dome, so an opaque splat canopy would blind the pilot.
  Panes use a `transmittance` of 0.7: per-splat alpha is `1 - T^(1 / (2 pi k^2))` (about 0.029) so the
  overlapping sheet lets 70 % through; from outside they read as dark tinted glass over the dark cockpit
  well, the hoops and rim are opaque frame. This is the one deviation from "opacity 0.98" in section 9.1.
  `(H)`: check both views; if GDGS's `alpha_cutoff` drops the panes entirely, raise `transmittance` or the
  overlap for the glass material.

## Determinism and options

Everything comes from `ctx.rng("splats", <part>)` streams (one per part, so adding a part never reshuffles
another) and from pure geometry; two builds with the same seed are byte-identical (checked by SHA-256 in
`test_starfury_aurora.py` and verified with `scripts/gen_assets.sh` run twice). Options
(`BuildContext.options`, CLI `--spacing` / `--option`): `spacing` (m, 0.035), `overlap` (k, 1.4),
`thickness` (normal sigma / s, 0.03). The AC3 budget warning only fires at the default spacing.

(bell lips, pod radius, muzzle bore), OBJ validity and bounds, cockpit mesh around the camera, SHA-256
determinism, option behaviour.

## Known limits / for the human tester `(H)`

- No ambient occlusion in the bake (strut roots, the bell interiors): add a BVH-based AO pass if the hull
  looks flat. The two-tone look (key vs. shadow) carries most of the shape today.
- Interior splats exist where parts overlap (strut tips inside pods, dome base inside the hull): invisible
  behind opaque splats, about 1 % of the budget.
- Judge: continuity of the nose in the cockpit view, panel-line crispness (tune `overlap`), canopy tint from
  inside and outside, the mesh/splat lighting mismatch between the dim cockpit interior and the baked hull.
